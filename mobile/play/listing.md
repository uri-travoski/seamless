## Full description (≤ 4000)

Seamless for Android is the phone app for your own Seamless server — self-hosted webmail that brings all your mailboxes together.

You need a Seamless server (run by you or your organisation). Enter its address once, sign in with your Seamless email and password, and your mail is there.

WHAT YOU GET
• One inbox for all mailboxes, or switch to a category or a single mailbox from the top-right selector
• A familiar, fast list: bold unread, stars, attachment clips, coloured labels, swipe to archive or delete with Undo
• Search with operators such as from:, has:attachment and label:, plus search options for dates and size
• Conversation view with a locked-down message viewer: remote images stay blocked until you allow them, and links show their real address before you open them
• Write, reply, forward and save drafts; attach files; schedule a send; undo a send
• Labels you can create, colour, nest and share
• Notifications for new mail and critical alerts (a mailbox that stopped working), with Mark as read, Archive, Delete and Reply right from the notification
• Option to hide message content in notifications
• Light and dark themes that follow your phone

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your mail lives on your own server. The app talks only to that server — no ads, no analytics, no tracking, nothing sent to the app's developer. Push notifications use Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging only if your administrator turns it on; otherwise the app checks for mail periodically.

Plain http is accepted only for servers on your home network; everywhere else the connection must use https.

Seamless is not a mail provider. Ask your administrator for the server address.
