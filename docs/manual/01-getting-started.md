# Getting started

Seamless is a self-hosted, AI-native webmail app. It unifies all your email accounts into one inbox, files mail automatically, and keeps only what is worth keeping. This manual covers every feature — use the list on the left to jump around.

## First run

The very first account you create becomes the **admin**. Pick an email and password on the login screen and you are in. Admins can add more users under **Settings > Users**; each user has their own accounts, labels, filters, AI configuration, and mail — nothing is shared between users.

## Connect your first account

Nothing shows up until you connect a mailbox. Open **Settings > Accounts > Add account** and enter your IMAP/SMTP details, or connect **Gmail** or **Microsoft** with OAuth. See **Accounts & providers** for the full walkthrough. Once connected, Seamless syncs in the background and mail appears within seconds.

## The layout

- **Sidebar** (left) — Compose, the fetch button, your mailboxes, and your labels.
- **Message list** (middle) — the mail in the current view.
- **Reading pane** (right) — the open conversation.

Two layouts are available (switch in **Settings > General**, or with the layout button above the list):

- **Split view** — list beside the reading pane.
- **Table view** — a full-width Gmail-style list; clicking a row opens the message.

## The sidebar

- **Compose** — start a new message. The small cloud button to its left **checks all accounts for new mail** on demand.
- **Inbox, Starred, Scheduled, Drafts, Sent, Archive, Junk, Trash** — your unified mailboxes. Every connected account's Inbox is merged into one, and each account gets its own **color bar** down the left edge of a row so you can tell them apart.
- **Review** — appears only when the AI is unsure about some mail and wants a quick decision from you.
- **Labels** — your colored labels, across all accounts. Click the **+** on the right of the LABELS heading to add one; click the **color dot** in front of a label to recolor it.
- **Accounts** — expand to see each account's own folders.

## The philosophy

- **Delete by default, but never silently.** Everything gets a disposition. Mail on a countdown shows an orange badge (`10d`). Deletions go to Trash first with a grace window; protected labels are never touched; every action is logged.
- **Safe to keep too long, never safe to delete too early.**
- **Every correction teaches it.** Relabel a message and similar mail lands right next time. You can also **Teach the AI** in plain English (see that page).
