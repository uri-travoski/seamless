# Notifications

## The bell

The bell in the top-right corner shows how many notifications are unread and, when clicked, the latest six: new mail, someone opening your email, send failures, mailboxes that can't sync, backups, security changes. Repeats collapse into one line ("Opened 5×"). Click a notification to go straight to it — an open goes to the **open report**, new mail to the message. **Mark all read** clears the badge (in every open tab); the gear opens the settings.

## All notifications

**See all notifications** opens the full list, with filters that stay in the address bar (so you can bookmark a view):

- **All / Unread / Read**
- **Type** — New mail, Opened, Reminders, Follow-ups, Snooze, Send failures, Sync errors, Backups, Security, Leads, Assignments
- **Mailbox or category**
- **Date** — today, last 7 or 30 days, or a custom range
- **Search** in the title and text

Choose 25, 50 or 100 per page. Tick notifications to mark them read, unread, or delete them.

## Settings > Notifications

- A row per type with **In-app** (the bell), **Push** (your phone or desktop) and **Email digest**.
- **Mute mailboxes** — a muted mailbox still gets mail, it just doesn't notify.
- **Quiet hours** — push is held between the times you set (Perth time); the bell still fills up.
- **Sound** — a short chime when something new arrives.

Push needs to be enabled once per device in **Settings > General**. "New sign-in" notifications are off by default; turn them on here if you want one every time someone signs in.
